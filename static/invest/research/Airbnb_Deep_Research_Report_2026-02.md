## 1. Business Overview

Airbnb connects hosts and travelers and earns service fees on transactions. Gross booking value (GBV) is the value of reservations, while revenue reflects the fees earned under the company's recognition policy. This timing matters because Reserve Now, Pay Later can schedule collection later in the booking cycle. The product expansion into services, experiences and hotels can raise booking frequency. The company has yet to demonstrate how much these categories contribute to recurring revenue and margins. [Q3 shareholder letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1559720/000119312526449727/d437988dex991.htm) · [FY2025 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1559720/000155972026000004/abnb-20251231.htm)

## 2. Industry & Competitive Position

Airbnb's differentiated supply, brand, reviews and host tools support its direct channel. Booking.com and Expedia compete through broader inventory, loyalty and distribution. Hosts can list on several platforms. The turnover risk is whether travelers keep starting their search on Airbnb as third-party agents improve, and whether Airbnb can sustain supply in cities with tighter short-term rental rules. Q3 bookings rose 16%, up from 10% in Q2. That supports current demand. It gives limited evidence on structural market share or the economics of new distribution channels. [Q3 shareholder letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1559720/000119312526449727/d437988dex991.htm) · [FY2025 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1559720/000155972026000004/abnb-20251231.htm)

## 4. Management Assessment

Under Brian Chesky, the product agenda centers on direct demand, localization and adjacent categories. The Q3 beat and higher full-year margin guide support the execution case. The improvement in net income also reflects lower taxes. The incremental test is whether these investments produce repeat demand and durable margins while repurchases absorb dilution at rational prices. The 20-vote B structure concentrates control, so the capital-allocation record matters for minority holders. [Q3 shareholder letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1559720/000119312526449727/d437988dex991.htm) · [FY2025 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1559720/000155972026000004/abnb-20251231.htm)

## 5. Bull Case

Reported Q3 growth of 19% and FX-neutral growth of 16% show demand accelerated from Q2's 13% ex-FX. Scale, repeat bookings and better product conversion could sustain mid-teens growth, with higher adj. EBITDA margin as incremental revenue exceeds support and marketing costs. The bull case puts FY2028 revenue at $19.26B, margin at 37.5% and terminal value at $249.57 per share, or +19.35% annualized. That path requires continuing demand and a 32x exit multiple. [Q2 shareholder letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1559720/000119312526337928/d70413dex991.htm) · [Q3 shareholder letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1559720/000119312526449727/d437988dex991.htm)

## 6. Bear Case

Third-party agents could shift discovery away from Airbnb and raise distribution costs. Local restrictions could limit inventory, and platform fee changes can squeeze monetization even while GBV grows. The Q3 letter discusses Airbnb's own AI efforts. The earnings call has yet to be verified for third-party attribution. The bear path assumes 7% / 5% revenue growth in 2027 / 2028, a 33.5% 2028 adj. EBITDA margin and an 18x exit, giving $104.58 per share or −21.13% annualized. [Q3 shareholder letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1559720/000119312526449727/d437988dex991.htm) · [Q3 Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1559720/000155972026000032/abnb-20260930.htm)

## 7. Key Uncertainties

- Q4 growth guidance includes currency help and follows a quarter with unusually strong demand. Repeatable ex-FX growth into 2027 carries more weight for the scenario than the headline guide alone.
- FY2026 margin of about 36% remains guidance. Q4 marketing is expected to weigh on the quarter, and incremental services monetization remains unquantified.
- Selling H shares outside the subsidiary, equity grants, execution prices for repurchases and interest earned on customer balances can change the cash and EPS bridge. Company and customer liquidity have separate economics.
- The Q3 filing reports no material change in risk factors from the annual and prior interim reports and still discloses the disputed proposed Spanish fine of about €65M. The operating evidence has a different scope from a full legal or call transcript review. [Q3 Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1559720/000155972026000032/abnb-20260930.htm) · [Q3 shareholder letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1559720/000119312526449727/d437988dex991.htm)
