**2026-07-23 monitoring update (no stance change):** Digital Realty's official Q2 2026 results (8-K Exhibit 99.1, 2026-07-23) reported Core FFO/share (excluding net promote) of $2.13 (Q1 was $2.04; 2Q25 was $1.87) and cash renewal rate increases of +25.4%. The company signed $307M of annualized GAAP base-rent bookings at 100% share / $208M at DLR share, including a record $108M from the 0-1MW plus interconnection category, or 52% of DLR-share bookings. Quarter-end signed-but-not-commenced backlog rose to a company-stated record $1.9B at 100% share / $1.4B at DLR share, but the DLR-share backlog included about $380M of incremental backlog from the acquisition of a 64% interest in three Northern Virginia assets; the record therefore cannot be attributed entirely to organic quarterly leasing. The Q2 supplemental also raised the capacity frame from about 3GW in place plus about 6GW future development to about 3GW in place plus about 9GW future development. Management raised 2026 Core FFO/share (excluding net promote) guidance from $8.00-8.10 to $8.15-8.20 and the Constant-Currency measure to $8.10-8.15. As a facility-interconnection-leasing reading, record 0-1MW/interconnection bookings, their 52% mix, renewal spreads and the shorter commencement lag support broader network-dense deployment; they do not prove that all backlog growth was organic or AI-specific. Evidence limits: quarterly bookings stepped down materially from Q1's $707M/$423M (Q1 was an unusually large quarter), single-quarter bookings are lumpy, acquired backlog must be separated from new leasing, and commencement still depends on power/permitting and capital. That monitoring update did not refresh the price snapshot; the separate current-price valuation review it called for was completed on 2026-07-31 at the 2026-07-30 close and moved the stance to neutral-watch / medium conviction.

These are the items that should drive future reruns. A monitoring update should change the report only when it changes the chain read-through or the valuation tolerance.
