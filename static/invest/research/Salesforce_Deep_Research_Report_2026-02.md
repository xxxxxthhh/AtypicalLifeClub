## 1. Business Overview

### What Is Salesforce

Founded in 1999, Salesforce is the world's largest CRM software company and a pioneer of the SaaS model. It commands ~21-26% of the global CRM market — 4-5x the share of second-place Microsoft Dynamics. Added to the Dow Jones Industrial Average in 2024.

### How It Makes Money

Nearly all revenue (~95%) comes from subscriptions, across five cloud products:

**Five Core Clouds:**
- Sales Cloud (sales automation) — largest revenue contributor
- Service Cloud (customer service) — second largest
- Platform & Other (Data Cloud, Heroku, MuleSoft)
- Marketing & Commerce Cloud
- Tableau (analytics)

**New Growth Engines:**
- Agentforce (AI Agent platform) — ~~ARR $540M, +330% YoY~~ ARR $800M (+169% YoY, Q4 FY2026)
- Data Cloud / Data 360 (unified data platform) — ~~combined with Agentforce: $1.4B ARR~~ Data 360 ARR $1.1B; combined Agentforce & Data 360 ARR $2.9B+ (+200%+ YoY)

## 2. Industry & Competitive Position

### CRM Market Overview

Global CRM market ~$100-113B in 2025, projected to reach ~$263B by 2032 (CAGR ~13%). AI in CRM market worth ~$11B in 2025, expected to reach $48.4B by 2033.

### Competitive Landscape

| Competitor | Global CRM Share | Core Advantage | Threat to Salesforce |
|-----------|-----------------|----------------|---------------------|
| Microsoft Dynamics | ~5-7% | Office/Azure ecosystem | Medium-High |
| HubSpot | ~3-4% | SMB-friendly, ease of use | Medium |
| ServiceNow | N/A (ITSM) | Backend workflows + AI | Medium |
| Oracle/SAP | ~3-5% each | ERP integration | Low-Medium |

### Moat Assessment

**🏰 Strong Moat Factors:**

1. **Extremely High Switching Costs**
   - CRM is a core enterprise system; migration costs are enormous
   - Average customer lifecycle >10 years
   - Deep customization makes replacement nearly impossible
   - 50%+ of Fortune 500 use Data Cloud

2. **Ecosystem Network Effects**
   - AppExchange has 7,000+ third-party apps
   - Millions of certified Salesforce developers/admins globally
   - Massive partner ecosystem (Accenture, Deloitte, etc.)

3. **Brand & Market Position**
   - 21-26% CRM market share, 4-5x the second place
   - Dow Jones Industrial Average constituent
   - "CRM" is literally Salesforce's ticker — brand equals category

**🏚️ Weak Moat Factors:**

1. **Slowing Growth**
   - Revenue growth decelerated from +24% (FY22) to +9% (FY25)
   - Growth ceiling for large enterprise software becoming visible
   - Whether AI can re-accelerate growth remains uncertain

2. **Pricing Pressure**
   - 2025's 6% price hike drew customer pushback
   - Low-cost alternatives like HubSpot eroding SMB share
   - Extra charges for AI features may face resistance

**Overall Moat Rating: Strong (🏰)**
Salesforce's moat derives primarily from extremely high switching costs and ecosystem lock-in — among the strongest in enterprise software.
