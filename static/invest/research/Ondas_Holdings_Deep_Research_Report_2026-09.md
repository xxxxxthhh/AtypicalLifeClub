## 3. Industry, Competition and Moat <!-- report-module:competition -->

Ondas competes for government and industrial programs where qualification, field performance and the ability to deliver and maintain equipment matter. The purchased portfolio broadens its access to customers and programs. Integration costs, purchase accounting and DZYNE's losses constrain the evidence for durable pricing power. The issuer treats the $982M Mistral IDIQ as a contract ceiling and describes shipments against orders under it. The November 10 Digital Bat release confirms a purchase order and describes the ARAN acquisition as planned. Model revenue depends on delivery and acceptance. A scalable product and service portfolio earns the upper exit multiple only in the bull case. The base and bear cases discount Ondas for integration and profitability risk. The multiples in §8 are scenario assumptions. The September peer snapshots belong to the initial coverage date.

## 6. Bull and Bear Cases <!-- report-module:bullBear -->

- **Bull.** Backlog converts on schedule, the existing portfolio exceeds the FY2027 target, and repeat sales and integration improve gross margin. The lower entry quote offers substantial upside if profitability supports a 4.5x exit revenue multiple with no additional capital raise.
- **Base.** Ondas reaches the FY2026 floor and grows through the delivery ramp but misses the initial FY2027 target. Cash conversion improves gradually, and the business still needs to absorb earn-outs and stock compensation. A 2.5x exit multiple allows for growth and persistent execution risk.
- **Bear.** Deliveries slip, weak margins and working capital keep cash flow negative, and a below-anchor equity issuance funds continued operations. Contract wins and revenue growth coexist with falling value per share. The downside includes refinancing and goodwill risk as assumptions.

## 10. Conclusion <!-- report-module:conclusion -->

The proposed change from cautious to **bearish-avoid, medium conviction** follows the verified original dilution condition and a fresh valuation. Q3 sales and guidance support upside, but the 35% bear-case weighting, its 69.80% value loss and $140.648M Q3 operating cash outflow make the weighted return fragile. A credible reopening would require a current share and financing bridge, sustained delivery and cash conversion, and a fresh scenario grid with improved downside economics. The factual trigger predates the proposed stance date. Independent counter-calculation and owner decision remain pending.
