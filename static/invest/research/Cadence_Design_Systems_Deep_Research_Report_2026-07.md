**2026-07-27 monitoring update (no stance change):** Cadence's official Q2 2026 results (8-K Exhibits 99.01/99.02, 2026-07-27) reported revenue of $1,584.5M (+24.2% YoY vs $1,275.4M in Q2 2025), GAAP operating margin 28.4%, non-GAAP operating margin 45.5%, GAAP diluted EPS $1.33, and non-GAAP diluted EPS $2.11. Quarter-end backlog was a company-stated record $8.1B (roughly flat vs Q1's $8.0B), and revenue expected within 12 months from remaining performance obligations was $4.2B (Q1 was $4.0B). Core EDA / Semiconductor IP / System Design & Analysis revenue grew 18% / more than 40% / 37% YoY; IP growth was attributed to Star IP, a significant Intel agreement, and other leading-semiconductor wins, while SDA growth was attributed to PCB, advanced packaging, and Hexagon D&E integration. Management raised FY2026 guidance to about 19% YoY revenue growth ($6.26-6.34B), $8.10 non-GAAP EPS at the midpoint ($8.05-8.15), and about $2B operating cash flow; Q3 revenue guidance is $1.595-1.625B. As a semicap-eda-capex-commitment reading, EDA/IP backlog and RPO did not diverge from rising downstream capex, reducing but not eliminating design-completion risk. Evidence limits: backlog was roughly flat QoQ, gross backlog additions/burn were not disclosed, and the company did not quantify agentic-workflow revenue or split Q2 IP growth among HBM, PCIe, or SerDes. The July 2 valuation snapshot used the then-current Q1 guide and is not recomputed here; cautious / medium conviction remains for a separate current-price valuation review.

These are the items that should drive future reruns. A monitoring update should change the report only when it changes the chain read-through or the valuation tolerance.
