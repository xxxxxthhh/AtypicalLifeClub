## Executive Summary <!-- report-module:overview -->

**One-line thesis:** Zhongji Innolight has ranked first in global optical-interconnect solution **revenue** for five consecutive years on CIC's measure, and its first half of 2026 shows three curves rising together — units +109.8%, derived blended ASP +36.6%, module gross margin 46.59% — confirming that AI cluster expansion reaches the physical link layer. But rebuilt against the RMB 908.90 close on 2026-09-09, the probability-weighted expected return is only **5.0%–6.6% annualized**, below an 8%–10% required return, and the balance sheet carries a redemption liability that grew RMB 4.157B in the half to RMB 5.057B.

**Investment judgment:** **Cautious / medium conviction.** Market capitalization is **RMB 1,070.60B** (RMB 908.90 × 1,177,909,641 shares); TTM (2025Q3–2026Q2) revenue of RMB 65.229B and parent net profit of RMB 20.453B give **52.3x TTM P/E and 16.4x TTM P/S**. Separating the **earnings-growth horizon** (the Q2-annualized run rate, centred mid-2026, to FY2029 — 3.5 years) from the **holding period** (2026-09-09 to 2029-12-31 — **3.31 years**): at a 25x exit multiple and an 8%–10% required return, today's price implies parent net profit compounding **17.2%–19.3%** from the Q2-annualized **RMB 31.667B**, against a base case of **15.6%**. More decisively, weighting the three scenarios 30/40/30 gives terminal equity of **RMB 1,259.3–1,322.4B**, or **5.0%–6.6% annualized** over 3.31 years — **1.4 to 5.0 points** below the hurdle. In sensitivity testing, widening the bull or the bear exit multiple alone still falls short; only widening both together reaches 6.8%–8.3%, whose upper end clears 8% but remains below 10%. That is a negative skew, not a neutral one.

**Chain job:** This report is the `optical` layer's **architecture check**, and that check is **currently unresolved**. Coherent already carries the layer's quarterly order-velocity dashboard; this report's comparative advantage is that it is the only one in the book disclosing **aggregate optical-transceiver capacity, output and unit sales**, allowing an aggregate blended ASP to be derived, alongside a complete pluggable / XPO / NPO / CPO / OCS roadmap. **Note: that volume/price basis is an aggregate measure, not a pluggable-only one** — the issuer provides no form-factor split. The evidence boundary must be stated plainly: **the interim discloses only aggregate optical-transceiver volumes, with no split by pluggable / NPO / CPO or by scenario (scale-out, scale-up, scale-across)**. This period therefore confirms only that aggregate module demand is real; it does **not** establish that pluggables are winning in scale-up. The test's observable criterion is filed as the monitoring item `copackaged-vs-pluggable-mix`, awaiting a disclosure basis.

## 1. Business Overview and Chain Role <!-- report-module:business -->

The company describes itself as "a leading global provider of optical interconnect solutions"; in substance it is a single-product business. Optical transceiver modules generated **RMB 41.355B** in the first half of 2026, or **99.0%** of revenue; the remainder — TO-CAN packages, optical sub-assemblies (OSA) and fiber-to-the-vehicle (FTTV) automotive optics — totalled **RMB 422.7M** (+18.3%). The product line spans 100G to 1.6T in OSFP, QSFP-DD and coherent ZR/ZR+ form factors. In the first half, **1.6T silicon-photonics modules entered volume ramp with shipments climbing quarter by quarter**, and the company calls them the core driver of revenue growth — but the interim report **does not disclose 1.6T revenue or its share of the mix**.

The decomposition convention must be stated: module revenue growth is **multiplicative**, `(1 + 109.8%) × (1 + 36.6%) − 1 ≈ 186.6%`, and the two legs carry an interaction term — they are **not two equal halves**. Measured as log shares, volume accounts for roughly **70%** of the growth and blended ASP for roughly **30%**. The volume leg is demand and is extrapolable; the ASP leg comes from mix shift into 800G and 1.6T. Section 6 treats "the mix benefit cannot be renewed" as a **falsifiable bear assumption** rather than an established fact — the company itself flags R&D investment in **3.2T and higher speeds** in the interim, and describes coherent-Lite as "critical for 1.6T and 3.2T," so higher price tiers do exist on the roadmap. Whether a new mix benefit arrives in time and at sufficient scale to offset 800G price erosion is an open observable, not a known conclusion.

Manufacturing footprint: Suzhou, Tongling, Chengdu, Taiwan and Thailand. Among overseas assets, Singapore-based TeraHop Pte. carries RMB 6.297B, or 14.29% of net assets. On 2026-03-12 the company granted 1.10M reserved restricted shares at RMB 53.10 under its fourth incentive plan; three vesting tranches during the half totalled 4,116,307 shares at RMB 35.16–52.10. At period end the company held 530 patents including 253 invention patents, with 47 newly granted in the half.

## 5. Bull Case <!-- report-module:bullBear -->

**Core: three independent pieces of demand-side evidence point the same way, capacity has already expanded ahead of them, and the H-share raise has funded expansion well beyond operating need.**

1. **Volume is ramping, not just price.** First-half unit sales were 18.99M, up 109.8%, and half-year capacity of 22.15M units already equals 79% of the whole of FY2025's 28.06M. This is direct evidence of optical modules being consumed inside AI clusters — not an order-book figure.
2. **Share is concentrating.** Over the same period Eoptolink grew 100.34% and TFC 15.15%, against Innolight's 182.49%. Nearly a two-fold growth gap under identical demand conditions says high-end share is concentrating at the top, and CIC's "first in revenue for five consecutive years" still holds through this acceleration.
3. **The company has bet on several architecture paths at once.** It was first to ship silicon-photonics 400G, 800G and 1.6T modules and first to develop coherent-Lite, and it is a founding participant in both the XPO MSA and the Open CPX MSA. At OFC 2026 it demonstrated a 12.8T 8xDR8 XPO module, a 6.4T 4xDR8 NPO module and a TFLN MZM-based 1.6T OSFP 2xLR4, and it lists 1.6T, 3.2T and higher speeds alongside NPO/XPO/CPO/OCS as R&D directions. Even if scale-up eventually migrates to co-packaged optics, the company sits on the standards-setting side — which limits the disruption of a path change, though it does not guarantee the margin travels with it.
4. **Expansion is already funded.** At 2026-06-30 cash equivalents were RMB 6.364B against just RMB 1.795B of borrowings and leases; even including the RMB 5.057B redemption liability, which puts the position at RMB 0.489B of net debt, the **RMB 45.707B** of confirmed H-share initial net proceeds received from July lifts pro-forma net cash to about **RMB 44.027B** (after the redemption liability, the RMB 873.7M Jones Tech first instalment and the RMB 318M of executed buyback), or about 4.1% of market capitalization, with 65% of the raise earmarked for R&D and global capacity. **The condition for this conclusion must be stated: it depends on counting the H-share proceeds. On the 30 June balance sheet alone the company is in modest net debt, not net cash, and a triggered redemption would consume that funding directly.**
5. **Buyback and dividend are running together.** The RMB 4.0–8.0B buyback carries a RMB 1,200 price cap above the current price and RMB 318M was executed on day one; the interim dividend is RMB 1.404B. Setting a repurchase cap above the current price is an observable statement by management.

**Upside valuation implication (not a price target):** if revenue compounds about 32% from the Q2-annualized RMB 89.126B through FY2029 (just below CIC's 33.8% datacom market CAGR) and net margin eases only slightly from the Q2-annualized 35.53% to 32%, FY2029 parent net profit would be about RMB 75.4B (+28.1% annualized); at a 28x exit multiple that is about RMB 2,110.2B of equity value, roughly **+22.8% annualized** over the 3.31-year holding period against RMB 1,070.60B today. This scenario requires gross and net margins to hold and is the most assumption-sensitive of the three.

## 6. Bear Case <!-- report-module:bullBear -->

**Core: this is a high-margin income statement priced at growth-stock multiples, with margin, customer structure, cash conversion and geopolitics all fragile at once — and the probability-weighted return rebuilt at today's price sits below the required return.**

1. **Net margin is at the top of the verified span, and its source is contested.** Net margin was 20.28% / 21.67% / 28.24% / **32.68%** in 2023 / 2024 / 2025 / 2026 H1 — up 12.40 points in three years, about +61% relative, **not a doubling** — and module gross margin of 46.59% is the highest across the 2024–2026H1 span this report verified (the complete earlier series was not retrieved). **Bear assumption (falsifiable): the expansion came mainly from supply tightness rather than durable efficiency gains.** The counter-evidence must be recorded too — the company attributes the improvement in part to "continuously optimized product solutions and improving operating efficiency," and discloses no cost bridge, so a zero efficiency contribution is not established. A return to FY2025's 42.61% is a 3.98-point gross-margin gap, which at the period's roughly 15% effective tax rate cuts net margin by about 3–4 points; a return to 2025 H1's 39.96% is a 6.63-point gap, or roughly 5–6 points.
2. **Whether the mix benefit can be renewed is unresolved.** About 30% of the half's module revenue growth came from blended-ASP uplift on a log-share basis. **Bear assumption (falsifiable): once 1.6T is the bulk of shipments, the next price tier ramps too slowly to offset 800G erosion.** The counter-evidence again belongs on the page: the company lists **3.2T and higher speeds** among its R&D directions and describes coherent-Lite as "critical for 1.6T and 3.2T," so higher tiers exist on the roadmap. This report does not claim there is no higher tier — only that the timing and magnitude are unknown and the company provides no quantified price or mix bridge.
3. **Customer concentration is rising, not falling.** The top five customers were **75.98%** of 2025 revenue (largest 24.06%). Top-five receivables and contract assets by debtor rose from **65.66%** at end-2025 to **72.49%** at 2026-06-30, an increase of **6.83 points** (the largest single debtor fell from 24.54% to 20.86%, but the top-five aggregate rose). Note the denominators differ: 75.98% is a revenue measure and 72.49% a receivables measure, and they must not be used interchangeably. The company does not name customers. An architecture change or supplier switch at any one of them would rewrite the volume curve directly — precisely what the "customer concentration" clause of the `chip-vs-downstream` rule is meant to catch.
4. **Second-quarter operating cash flow was negative.** First-half operating cash flow was RMB 1.800B against RMB 13.651B of parent net profit, converting at 13.2%; backing out the Q1 report, **Q1 was RMB 3.368B (58.7%) and Q2 was −RMB 1.568B (−19.8%)** — deterioration accelerating within the half, not a stable working-capital draw. Inventory days (ending-balance, about 160) did improve, but receivable days (about 65) **worsened** against about 60 in 2025; and measured directly on balance growth, Q2 receivables rose **+57.32%** and inventory **+26.50%** against revenue's **+14.29%**. Working-capital absorption explains the cash-flow gap but **does not prove collection quality is sound**: no independent evidence supports that conclusion this period. Paying 52.3x earnings for profit whose most recent quarter converted negatively leaves thin tolerance.
5. **The redemption liability grew RMB 4.157B in six months.** Long-term payables rose from RMB 0.900B to RMB 5.057B, identified in Hong Kong note 19 as the present value of TeraHop investors' repurchase rights. It turns the 30 June position from apparent net cash into **net debt of RMB 0.489B**, and it is USD-denominated (USD 742.5M), layering currency risk on top. The trigger-event terms are not disclosed in detail, so neither the probability nor the timing can be assessed.
6. **The geopolitical leg has moved from "listed" to "statutory consequences already scheduled."** On 2026-06-08 (US time) the US Department of War added the company to the **Section 1260H List**; on 2026-06-10 the company said the designation is inconsistent with the facts and has had no material effect on operations. What matters is that the consequences require no further escalation: **Section 805 of the FY2024 National Defense Authorization Act (Pub. L. 118-31)** provides that the Secretary of Defense may not enter into, renew or extend a contract to procure goods, services or technology **with** a 1260H-listed entity or an entity it controls, **effective 2026-06-30**; and may not enter into, renew or extend a contract for goods or services that **include** goods or services produced or developed by such an entity, **effective 2027-06-30**. The section carries three limits: it does not reach contracts entered into before the relevant effective date (including their modification, extension or renewal), it does not apply to "components" as defined in 41 U.S.C. §105, and the Secretary may grant a waiver on request. **So the 94.8% overseas revenue share cannot be equated with affected revenue** — the disclosed customers are cloud service providers and AI compute providers, and the transmission path exists only through whatever portion of those customers' business supplies the US Department of Defense, a magnitude the company does not disclose and this report cannot estimate. The real objects of observation are the implementing rules for the 2027-06-30 indirect prohibition, customers' defense-procurement exposure, and whether the company or its customers seek waivers.

**Downside valuation implication (not a price target):** if 2027–2028 brings a hyperscaler capex digestion phase while NPO/CPO takes part of the scale-up socket, revenue compounds only 8% from RMB 89.126B through FY2029 and net margin falls from 35.53% to 18%, FY2029 parent net profit would be about RMB 21.0B — a **decline** against the current run rate, −11.1% annualized; at a 16x exit multiple that is about RMB 336.0B of equity value, roughly **−29.5% annualized** over 3.31 years. This scenario is conjunctive — growth, margin and multiple must all break together — so it is not the likely case, but if it happens the loss is permanent rather than a drawdown.

**What is priced in, and the expectation gap:**

The reverse arithmetic: starting from RMB 1,070.60B of market capitalization on 2026-09-09, an investor demanding 8%–10% annually and holding **3.31 years** to end-2029 needs an exit equity value of **RMB 1,381.2–1,467.7B**. Converting that into required FY2029 parent net profit at different exit multiples and comparing with the Q2-annualized RMB 31.667B over the 3.5-year earnings horizon gives the market-implied net-profit CAGR: **exiting at 20x requires 25.0%–27.1%; at 25x requires 17.2%–19.3%; at 30x requires 11.3%–13.2%.** Taking the 25x centre, **today's price implies roughly 17%–19% annual compounding in parent net profit**, against this report's base case of **15.6%** — an expectation gap of **−1.6 to −3.7 points**.

But what settles the stance is not that gap; it is the **probability-weighted expected return**: 30%×2,110.2 + 40%×(1,313.7–1,471.3) + 30%×336.0 = **RMB 1,259.3–1,322.4B**, which against RMB 1,070.60B over 3.31 years is **5.0%–6.6% annualized** — **1.4 to 5.0 points below** the 8%–10% required return. Sensitivity testing shows the conclusion is robust: widening the bear exit multiple from 16x to 20x gives 5.7%–7.2%, and lifting the bull exit multiple from 28x to 30x gives 6.2%–7.7% — each on its own still below 8%. Only widening both together reaches 6.8%–8.3%: that upper end does clear the 8% floor of the hurdle, though it remains below the 10% top, and it already takes the most favourable value of three assumptions at once. To push the weighted return durably above 8% the weights would have to move to roughly 40% bull / 40% base / 20% bear — that is, a four-in-ten chance the company compounds revenue at 32% on a 32% net margin for three and a half years, against only two-in-ten for a digestion phase. With net margin at the top of the verified span, second-quarter operating cash flow negative, top-five receivables concentration rising, and the §805 direct prohibition already effective since 2026-06-30, this report does not consider that weighting defensible.

## 10. Conclusion <!-- report-module:conclusion -->

**(1) Chain-validation job.** In the `optical` layer this report carries the **architecture check**, and that check is **unresolved this period**. What is confirmed: first-half unit sales of 18.99M (+109.8%), derived blended ASP +36.6% and module gross margin of 46.59% give the optical layer a **positive confirmation at the aggregate module-demand level** — so for the `chip-vs-downstream` rule, the antecedent that networking, optical and facility orders do not confirm does not hold this period. What is not confirmed is that pluggables are winning in scale-up: the interim supplies aggregate volumes and a **revenue** ranking only, with no pluggable/NPO/CPO split and no scenario breakdown. The architecture check's criterion is filed as the monitoring item `copackaged-vs-pluggable-mix`, to be adjudicated when a disclosure basis appears.

**(2) Expectation gap.** At RMB 908.90 (52.3x TTM, 39.2x H1-annualized, 33.8x Q2-annualized), a 25x exit at an 8%–10% required return over a 3.31-year hold implies roughly **17%–19%** annual parent-net-profit compounding, against a base case of **15.6%** — a gap of −1.6 to −3.7 points.

**(3) Stance and conviction.** **Cautious / medium conviction.** The stance is read directly off the §8 grid: weighting 30% bull / 40% base / 30% bear gives equity value of RMB 1,259.3–1,322.4B, or **5.0%–6.6% annualized** over 3.31 years — 1.4 to 5.0 points below the 8%–10% required return; widening the bull or the bear exit multiple alone still falls short (5.7%–7.7%), and only widening both together reaches 6.8%–8.3%, whose upper end clears 8% but not 10%. This is not "skew near zero"; it is a **clearly negative skew**, which is why the stance is cautious rather than neutral-watch. Why not `bearish-avoid`: the base case still delivers 6.4%–10.1% annualized, and the business is improving rather than deteriorating — the problem is the price. Conviction is medium: the fact base is unusually complete (volume, price, gross margin, customer concentration and standalone-quarter cash conversion are all disclosed first-hand by the issuer) and the negative-skew conclusion survives sensitivity testing, but the terminal net margin rests on a single half-year observation and the error band remains wide.

**(4) Upgrade / downgrade triggers.** **Upgrade to neutral-watch or constructive** if the 2026 annual report confirms module gross margin still above 44% with unit sales still roughly doubling year over year, operating cash flow back above 50% of parent net profit on a standalone-quarter basis, top-five receivables concentration no longer rising, the redemption liability no longer expanding, and the §805 indirect prohibition confirmed not to cover the company's products. **Downgrade to bearish-avoid** if module gross margin falls below 40% (or the consolidated margin derived from the Q3 report drops more than three points sequentially), half-year unit-sales growth falls below 30% year over year, standalone-quarter operating cash flow is negative for two consecutive quarters, the redemption liability is triggered or expands materially further, or the §805 indirect prohibition is confirmed to cover the company's products and any customer above 10% of revenue explicitly reallocates supply share.
