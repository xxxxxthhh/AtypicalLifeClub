## 4. The 2026 "Software-mageddon": What Happened?

### Crash Timeline

- **Jan 15, 2026:** 50-day MA crosses below 200-day MA ("death cross")
- **Late Jan - Early Feb 2026:** Seven consecutive down days, -16% in seven sessions
- **Feb 2026:** Anthropic releases AI legal automation tool, triggering AI substitution panic across the software sector
- **Feb 13, 2026:** IGV closes at $82.77, YTD -23.4%

### Root Causes

**1. AI Disruption Panic (Primary Driver)**
- Market fears that AI agents will automate tasks traditionally performed by existing software
- Anthropic's legal AI tool was the direct catalyst, sparking selloffs in vertical software companies like RELX and Wolters Kluwer
- Panic spread to the entire SaaS sector

**2. Per-Seat Licensing Model Under Threat**
- Traditional SaaS charges per user ("per-seat licensing")
- If AI agents replace human users, "seat count" declines, directly impacting revenue models
- Market began repricing all per-seat SaaS companies

**3. "AI Winners vs. AI Losers" Divergence**
- Capital flowing out of "potentially AI-disrupted" software companies
- Flowing into AI infrastructure (chips, cloud) and "AI-native" companies
- However, selling was indiscriminate — even AI beneficiaries like Palantir were hit

**4. Valuation Reset**
- Software sector valuations were already stretched in 2024-2025
- AI panic became the catalyst for multiple compression
- M&A and IPO activity stalled due to valuation uncertainty

## 9. Practical Investor Guidance

### Valuation and Scenario Grid (Update 2026-10-02)

**Method.** An ETF has no balance sheet or share count of its own to value. Per-unit value is the holdings' earnings times the multiple the market pays for them, less fees. Two earnings frames are used, and each is labelled:
- **Fund data (iShares):** P/E 35.13 as of 2026-09-30. iShares defines each holding's P/E as the latest closing price over the latest fiscal year's EPS, and excludes negative P/E ratios. This is a trailing basis that leaves loss-makers out.
- **This report's estimate:** a holdings-weighted forward P/E built from Nasdaq's yearly consensus EPS (retrieved 2026-10-02).
  - **Coverage:** the 30 largest holdings in the iShares 2026-09-30 holdings file, 87.41% of weight. MSTR, CRCL and BMNR are excluded because their earnings are driven by digital-asset marks, and RBRK because its EPS is negative. That leaves 83.72% covered. The uncovered 16.28% is assumed to earn the covered average yield.
  - **Calendarization:** each holding's fiscal years are converted to calendar years by month overlap. For CY2028, Sep-Nov year-ends (ADBE, SNPS, PTC) are treated as calendar years.
  - **Aggregation:** the aggregate is the weight-averaged earnings yield (a harmonic P/E) at 2026-09-30 holdings prices. It is converted to per-unit EPS with the 2026-09-30 NAV of $106.40.
  - **Mixed basis:** the feed mixes accounting bases. CRWD (FY Jan-2027 EPS $0.08), PANW (FY Jul-2027, $2.31) and DDOG (FY Dec-2026, $0.70) look GAAP, while CRM ($12.82) and ADBE ($20.01) look adjusted. Levels are therefore specific to this basis. The grid applies exit multiples on the same basis, so it is internally consistent, but it is not comparable with "forward P/E" figures published by other providers.
